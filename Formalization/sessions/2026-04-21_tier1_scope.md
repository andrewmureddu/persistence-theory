# Session log — 2026-04-21: Tier-1 computational scope for Predictions 8 and 9

## What I worked on

I resolved OP-9: the old tracker said "scope the Tier-1 computational tests for Predictions 8 and 9," but the active appendix still used "Tier 1" as a broad recommendation rather than an operational research plan.

## Why this was the right frontier

The project had already decided that the fastest empirical traction should come from the computational predictions, but one practical ambiguity remained:

1. The appendix named Tier 1 without saying what makes a prediction Tier 1 rather than Tier 2 or 3.
2. Prediction 9 was close to executable, but Prediction 8 still had a hidden modeling choice.
3. Without resolving that distinction, a future implementation pass could easily build the wrong simulator and then mistake a null result for a theory failure.

So this was a good continuation step: convert the tier language into an operational test design before any code gets written.
