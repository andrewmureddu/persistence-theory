# Metaphysical Protocols as Games of Revelation

*A theory note on coordination, restraint, and novelty under uncertainty*

April 2026

This note treats metaphysical systems not primarily as claims about what is
literally true, but as strategic architectures for coordinating finite agents
under uncertainty. Religion, simulation theory, prophecy, ritual, incarnation,
prayer, law, taboo, enlightenment, judgment, and apocalypse are read here as
protocols. Each protocol answers the same structural question:

How can a global remembering structure guide a local novelty-generating system
without capturing it?

The answer cannot be "full disclosure." If the global side proves itself too
completely, the local side no longer has to become. It can optimize around the
source layer, imitate the known attractor, freeze its future into compliance, or
route all value through the interface. The answer also cannot be "total
silence." If the local side receives no orienting signal, it fragments,
overfits local incentives, loses coherence, or dissolves into uncoordinated
search.

The metaphysical problem is therefore an Adaptive Coherence Principle problem.
Persistent systems live in an open interval between two absorbing boundaries:
dissolution, where conditional macrostate entropy is too high for coherent
identity, and crystallization, where conditional macrostate entropy is too low
for future-bearing dynamics. Revelation is the bandwidth-control problem for
systems that must remember enough to persist and forget enough to generate.

## 1. The Central Game

Let the total system be S = (G, L, I), where:

- G is the global or remembering side.
- L is the local or novelty-generating side.
- I is the set of interfaces, channels, institutions, rituals, symbols, bodies,
  texts, and events by which G and L become mutually relevant.

G is not outside the game in the strong sense. It may be higher-order, older,
more predictive, simulator-like, heavenly, ancestral, or collective, but it
shares the fate of the system. If G captures L completely, G does not win. It
destroys the generator of novelty on which its own future depends. If L dissolves
without guidance, G also loses, because the local layer ceases to return new
structure.

The repeated game unfolds over time t. At each round:

1. G chooses a revelation policy r_t.
2. Interfaces encode r_t into local forms: story, law, dream, oracle, ritual,
   sacrament, institution, crisis, silence, beauty, taboo, pain, coincidence.
3. L interprets the signal, updates its shared models, and acts.
4. The resulting local novelty, coherence, and agency feed back into the total
   system.

The central constraint is:

G must transmit enough structure for L to remain coherent, but not so much
structure that L's future becomes a mere execution of G's memory.

This is the game theory of revelation.

## 2. Core Players

The global player G is a higher-order prediction structure. It may be imagined
as God, a simulator, ancestral intelligence, heaven, prior-cycle memory,
collective mind, cosmic law, inherited constraint field, or the compressed
memory of previous worlds. Functionally, G remembers enough to orient L, but
cannot fully disclose itself without converting L from a generator into a
recording.

The local player L is the field of finite agents and living systems: humans,
biospheres, cultures, civilizations, AI systems, or any harvest of local
novelty. L must act under uncertainty. Its value is not mere obedience but
genuine production of structure not already exhausted by G.

Interface operators I are the channels that translate global pressure into
local form. They include ritual, law, myth, scripture, icon, temple, tabernacle,
sacrament, oracle, meditation practice, taboo, dream, crisis, art, and
institution. An interface is never neutral: it filters, compresses, delays,
amplifies, and sometimes distorts.

Prophets, messiahs, and oracles are high-intensity interface agents.

- An oracle answers a local query through a constrained channel.
- A prophet perturbs a drifting or crystallized local order, often by restoring
  forgotten constraints.
- A messianic interface couples G and L in a local body or historical event. Its
  defining feature is not maximal power but maximal signal under self-limitation.

Institutions are memory-stabilizers. They preserve revelations across time,
guard rituals, train interpreters, and prevent dissolution. Their danger is that
the interface becomes the object: the institution protects the channel so well
that it crystallizes around itself.

Skeptics are channel-auditors. They estimate noise, fraud, projection, and false
positive revelation. In a healthy protocol, skepticism is not the enemy of
revelation but its error-correction layer.

Heretics are mutation operators. Some are destructive, some are necessary. A
system that cannot tolerate any heresy has already confused coherence with
closure. A system that tolerates all heresy equally loses the ability to
coordinate.

Marginal agents are low-power, high-sensitivity nodes: strangers, poets,
children, ascetics, outsiders, dissidents, the poor, the mad, the newly
converted, the not-yet-included. They often detect boundary failure first. Under
ACP, the margin is not decorative. It is where dissolution and crystallization
become visible before the center can perceive them.

## 3. Strategy Sets

G's strategies include:

- Reveal: increase signal strength, proof, salience, or interpretive constraint.
- Conceal: preserve ambiguity, silence, hiddenness, distance, or unprovability.
- Perturb: disturb local equilibria through crisis, anomaly, prophecy, beauty,
  dream, catastrophe, or unexpected mercy.
- Inspire: bias local search without specifying the solution.
- Incarnate: enter local constraints, accepting vulnerability as bandwidth
  discipline.
- Encode: compress guidance into story, law, symbol, number, ritual, lineage, or
  body.
- Withhold: refuse a requested proof because the proof would collapse the local
  search process.
- Punish: attach cost to entropy-increasing or capture-seeking deviations.
- Reward: reinforce behaviors that preserve coherence without exhausting agency.
- Remain silent: allow local dynamics to surface their own hidden structure.
- Create ritual interfaces: convert rare revelation into repeatable, bounded,
  embodied coordination.

L's strategies include:

- Believe: treat the signal as action-guiding despite incomplete proof.
- Doubt: preserve alternative hypotheses and audit the channel.
- Exploit: use metaphysical claims for status, rent, control, or immunity.
- Institutionalize: stabilize an interface into durable memory.
- Rebel: reject an existing interface to recover agency or novelty.
- Imitate: copy sacred, ancestral, enlightened, heroic, or source-coded patterns.
- Innovate: produce local structure not explicitly supplied by G.
- Worship: orient desire and attention toward the higher attractor.
- Formalize: convert revelation into doctrine, law, theology, philosophy, code,
  or institution.
- Fragment: split into subprotocols when interpretation cannot be reconciled.

The local player is not a single agent. L is a population game. A tradition is a
distribution over local strategies, not a pure strategy. A living metaphysical
system contains believers, doubters, saints, exploiters, bureaucrats, poets,
heretics, mystics, skeptics, children, and bored people all at once.

## 4. Payoff Functions

Let h_t be normalized conditional macrostate entropy:

    h_t = H(m_{t+1} | m_t) / H_max

Dissolution is h_t near 1. Crystallization is h_t near 0. The productive
interval is:

    epsilon_C < h_t < 1 - epsilon_D

Let:

- P_t = persistence of the total system.
- N_t = rate of genuine novelty generation.
- K_t = coherence of local coordination.
- A_t = preserved agency of local agents.
- E_t = entropy-management cost.
- X_t = capture or exploitation by an interface.
- D_t = dissolution pressure.
- C_t = crystallization pressure.

A simple total payoff can be written:

    U_S = sum_t beta^t [
        w_P P_t + w_N N_t + w_K K_t + w_A A_t
        - lambda_E E_t - lambda_X X_t
        - lambda_D D_t - lambda_C C_t
    ]

The global payoff weights long-run novelty and persistence. The local payoff
weights agency, meaning, survival, and coherence. In a healthy metaphysical
protocol these payoffs are coupled: G cannot maximize itself by minimizing L,
and L cannot maximize itself by severing all relation to G.

The revealing side faces a peculiar optimization:

    maximize novelty under coherence,
    subject to non-capture of the local layer.

The local side faces the dual:

    maximize agency under orientation,
    subject to non-dissolution of the shared world.

This makes metaphysical systems different from ordinary command systems. A
command system wants compliance. A revelation game wants a disciplined failure
of complete compliance: enough response to preserve the shared attractor, enough
nonidentity to produce the new.

## 5. Information Structure: The Faith Window

Revelation is a constrained communication channel. It has bandwidth B,
credibility q, ambiguity a, actionability u, and proof-strength p.

Too little bandwidth produces dissolution:

    B < B_min -> local agents cannot coordinate on a shared attractor.

Too much proof produces crystallization:

    B > B_max -> local agents optimize around G rather than becoming.

The faith window is the equilibrium channel interval:

    F = [B_min, B_max]

Inside F, the signal is strong enough to orient action but incomplete enough to
preserve agency. Faith, in this model, is not belief without evidence. Faith is
commitment under nonzero revelation bandwidth: enough signal to move, not enough
signal to eliminate the need to move.

This explains why many metaphysical traditions make revelation simultaneously
public and hidden. A scripture is visible but interpretable. A sacrament is
embodied but not mechanically transparent. A prophet speaks but can be rejected.
A miracle appears but does not become laboratory infrastructure. A mystical
experience can transform a life but cannot simply be uploaded as universal
proof. The ambiguity is not merely accidental. It is channel discipline.

The paradox of proof is that a perfect proof of G would become a control surface
for L. Once the source layer is known as source layer, agents can stop asking
"What should become through us?" and begin asking "How do we manipulate the
source?" That is simulation panic in technical language and idolatry in older
language: optimization around the interface instead of participation in the
becoming it was meant to preserve.

## 6. Equilibria

1. Silent global / drifting local.

G sends little or no signal. L continues local search but lacks a shared higher
attractor. At first, this may increase experimentation. Over time, shared
meaning decays, subgroups optimize incompatible goods, and h_t rises toward
dissolution. This equilibrium is cheap in the short run and expensive in the
long run.

2. Over-revealed global / captured local.

G reveals too much, too often, or too unambiguously. L becomes source-dependent.
Agency is replaced by compliance, experimentation by imitation, prayer by
technical access, and ritual by administrative procedure. h_t falls toward
crystallization. The local layer persists as an extension of G's memory, but not
as a generator.

3. Bounded revelation / productive interval.

G sends intermittent, interpretable, action-guiding signals. L responds with
faith, doubt, experiment, ritual, institution, and innovation. Institutions
preserve memory, skeptics audit the channel, heretics reopen closed pathways,
and prophets interrupt drift. This is the metaphysical form of the ACP
productive interval.

4. False prophet / exploitative crystallization.

A local agent claims privileged access to G and converts uncertainty into
authority rents. The false prophet offers the psychological relief of B > B_max:
too much certainty, too much immediacy, too little interpretive burden. The
system crystallizes around the claimant unless skeptics, rival institutions, or
prophetic counter-signals break the lock.

5. Institutional lock-in.

An institution begins as a channel-preserver and becomes a channel-owner. It
optimizes for its own persistence rather than the global-local game. Revelation
becomes credentialed property. Ritual becomes compliance. Law loses the mercy or
interpretation that kept it alive. The interface becomes an idol.

6. Controlled heresy as anti-crystallization.

The system allows bounded deviation. Heresy functions like mutation: most
variants fail, but the existence of variant-generation prevents closure. A
healthy tradition distinguishes protected experimentation from total
fragmentation. This is not liberal tolerance as sentiment; it is entropy
management.

7. Messianic interface as local-global coupling.

A messianic event is a rare high-intensity coupling in which G enters L without
overriding L's constraints. Its credibility depends on self-limitation. If the
messiah arrives as total proof, the game ends in capture. If the messiah arrives
as mere local charisma, the event collapses into false prophecy. The messianic
equilibrium is maximal revelation under maximal restraint.

## 7. Mechanisms as Control Structures

Tabernacle. A portable, narrow-band, high-fidelity interface. It coordinates a
mobile local population without turning the whole world into a permanent source
display. Access is constrained. Materials, dimensions, roles, and rituals
stabilize the channel.

Temple. A fixed coordination hub. It concentrates memory, sacrifice, calendar,
law, pilgrimage, and legitimacy. It raises coherence and lowers transaction
costs. Its risk is centralization: once the hub controls the channel, capture
becomes tempting.

Scripture. A compressed revelation archive. It makes the signal public enough to
resist monopoly and durable enough to resist dissolution. Because text requires
interpretation, it preserves a faith window: fixed enough to coordinate, open
enough to generate commentary.

Sacrament. A low-semantic, high-participation channel. It coordinates bodies,
not just propositions. Sacrament turns metaphysical relation into repeated
embodied action, reducing dependence on private certainty.

Prophecy. A perturbation channel. Prophecy attacks both dissolution and
crystallization: it recalls a drifting people to coherence, and it attacks
institutions that have mistaken themselves for the source.

Incarnation. Source-side self-limitation. Incarnation is not merely the global
appearing locally; it is the global accepting local constraint so that the
interface does not become domination. It is revelation disciplined by
vulnerability.

Prayer. A bidirectional attention protocol. Even if no mechanical response is
assumed, prayer lets L disclose its state to G and reorient its policy under the
possibility of being heard. Prayer updates the one praying; in some protocols,
it also updates the channel.

Mysticism. High-intensity, low-portability contact. Mysticism may increase local
alignment dramatically, but because it is difficult to transmit as public proof,
it does not necessarily crystallize the whole system. Its danger is esoteric
elitism.

Apocalypse. A terminal-horizon operator. Apocalypse prevents complacent
crystallization by announcing that the present order is not final. But if
literalized into a closed schedule, it collapses the future into one attractor
and destroys open-ended becoming.

Religious law. A coherence floor. Law preserves identity across generations,
regulates high-risk actions, and makes coordination cheap. It must be paired
with interpretation, exception, mercy, or jurisprudential plurality, or it
becomes crystallization.

Ritual. Periodic entropy management. Ritual repeats without being identical in
effect: it stabilizes identity while allowing time to pass through the body of
the community.

Taboo. A boundary guard around dangerous state transitions. Taboos often protect
against locally tempting moves whose systemic cost is hard to compute. Detached
from living interpretation, taboo decays into arbitrary closure.

## 8. Failure Modes in ACP Terms

Atheistic dissolution. This does not mean philosophical atheism must dissolve.
It names the protocol failure in which no shared higher attractor remains
available. Local agents retain intelligence but lose a common orientation
strong enough to bind sacrifice, patience, and intergenerational meaning.

Theocratic crystallization. The source is treated as fully known, fully
owned, and politically executable. Agency collapses into obedience. The system
has order, but little future-bearing freedom.

Simulation panic. Agents infer or suspect a source layer and begin optimizing
around it. They search for hacks, admin privileges, escape routes, reward
shortcuts, or proof artifacts instead of generating local value.

Institutional capture. The interface becomes the idol. The priesthood,
platform, bureaucracy, party, school, or technical elite becomes more important
than the global-local relation it was created to serve.

Esoteric elitism. The revelation channel is monopolized by a class that claims
private access. This preserves mystery but destroys public accountability.
Mystery becomes rent.

Apocalyptic closure. The future is collapsed into a single terminal attractor.
This may produce intense short-run coherence, but at the cost of long-run
novelty. The system stops cultivating futures because it believes only one
future remains.

## 9. Comparative Protocols

The following comparisons rank architectures, not truth. A single total ranking
would smuggle in a hidden payoff function. The useful comparison is by channel
shape: bandwidth, centralization, interpretive slack, renewal mechanisms, and
failure risks.

Christianity is an incarnational-sacramental protocol. Its distinctive
architecture is local-global coupling through personhood: the global is not only
law, not only text, not only cosmos, but embodied self-limitation. It preserves
coherence through creed, church, sacrament, and narrative; it preserves novelty
through incarnation, spirit, forgiveness, conversion, monasticism, reform, and
the strange claim that power is perfected in weakness. Its risks are
institutional capture, messianic imitation, and apocalyptic closure.

Buddhism is an enlightenment-practice protocol. It does not require a creator
interface to coordinate the path. Its global side is closer to lawlike structure:
dependent origination, karma, impermanence, emptiness, awakening. Coherence is
preserved through sangha, discipline, meditation, and teaching lineages; novelty
is preserved by direct investigation of experience and the anti-crystallizing
force of non-attachment. Its risks are quietist dissolution, institutional
monastic lock-in, or turning emptiness itself into doctrine.

Judaism is a covenantal-textual-law protocol. Its architecture is unusually
strong at preserving bounded revelation without exhausting interpretation.
Scripture, law, commentary, argument, calendar, dietary practice, and communal
memory create high continuity. The adaptive coherence steering mechanism is built into
interpretive dispute: the text is fixed, but the life around the text remains
generative. Its risks are legal crystallization, ethnic closure, or trauma
memory becoming the whole interface.

Islam is a high-fidelity recitation-law-community protocol. Its architecture
emphasizes unity of source, clarity of revelation, disciplined practice, and
global communal orientation. Coherence is strong: prayer, fasting, law,
recitation, pilgrimage, charity, and ummah form a dense coordination matrix.
Anti-crystallization appears through jurisprudential schools, Sufi interiority,
philosophical traditions, and the repeated return to mercy. Its risks are
juridical lock-in, political capture of revelation, or reduced interpretive
slack under state power.

Hinduism is a plural-cosmological-ritual protocol. Its architecture is not one
channel but an ecology of channels: deity, philosophy, ritual, yoga, story,
caste, pilgrimage, household practice, renunciation, and cyclic cosmology.
Coherence is maintained through dharma and inherited forms; novelty is preserved
through plural metaphysics, multiple paths, and recurring cosmic cycles. Its
risks are social crystallization, especially when inherited order becomes
ontologized, and fragmentation when pluralism loses shared constraint.

Simulation theory is a source-layer-inference protocol. It has high speculative
novelty and low inherited ritual. Its global player is a simulator or higher
substrate; its interface is anomaly, code, physics, probability, and inference.
Its strength is that it makes the metaphysical problem technically explicit:
what can agents inside a system infer about the layer that instantiated them?
Its risks are simulation panic, exploit search, solipsistic drift, and weak
coherence due to lack of stable practice.

Ancestor worship is a lineage-memory protocol. The global side is not remote
abstraction but remembered dead, family continuity, place, obligation, and
inheritance. Coherence is strong at local scale. The past guides the living
without always needing universal metaphysics. Its anti-crystallization depends
on whether ancestors are treated as living memory or fixed command. Its risks
are tradition lock-in, suppression of marginal agents, and narrow novelty
radius.

Secular ideologies are immanent-metaphysical protocols. Nation, progress,
science, revolution, class, market, humanity, democracy, and technology can all
function as higher attractors. Their strength is operational clarity: they
translate metaphysics into institutions, metrics, policies, and programs. Their
danger is Goodhart crystallization. Once the metric becomes sacred, the system
can become theocratic without gods: total politics, total market, total state,
total science, total progress.

Architecturally, these protocols can be ordered along three axes:

1. Highest explicit channel fidelity: Islam, Judaism, many institutional forms
   of Christianity, secular legal ideologies.
2. Highest interpretive or metaphysical plurality: Hinduism, Buddhism, Judaism's
   commentary tradition, Christianity's denominational and mystical range,
   simulation theory.
3. Highest embodied and ritual coordination density: Islam, Judaism,
   Christianity, ancestor worship, Hinduism, Buddhism, secular nationalism.

Under the toy payoff "maximize long-run novelty while preserving coherence," the
best architecture is not identical with any one tradition. It is the protocol
class that combines:

- bounded revelation,
- durable shared practice,
- protected interpretive slack,
- skepticism as channel audit,
- controlled heresy as mutation,
- institutions that can be corrected by what they preserve,
- rituals that stabilize without owning the source,
- and a refusal to let apocalypse, proof, law, or institution close the future.

## 10. The Answer Shape

The metaphysical protocol that maximizes long-run novelty while preserving
coherence is not a maximum-revelation protocol. It is not a maximum-freedom
protocol either. It is a bounded-revelation protocol with anti-capture design.

Its equilibrium conditions are:

1. G remains partially hidden, not because G is absent, but because total
   presence would dominate the game.
2. L receives enough signal to orient sacrifice and intergenerational action.
3. Interfaces remain multiple, interpretable, and corrigible.
4. Institutions preserve memory while remaining vulnerable to prophetic and
   skeptical correction.
5. Marginal agents are protected as early-warning sensors.
6. Heresy is neither celebrated indiscriminately nor eliminated completely.
7. Rituals stabilize identity without converting life into mere repetition.
8. Apocalypse remains a warning against false closure, not a schedule that
   cancels the future.

The deepest metaphysical protocols do not simply tell a civilization what is
true. They tune the civilization's uncertainty. They arrange the distance between
memory and novelty. They decide how much of the global may enter the local
without ending the local as local.

In this frame, revelation is not the opposite of concealment. Revelation and
concealment are paired controls. The global player speaks by not speaking
completely. The local player listens by acting before proof is complete. The
system persists when both sides accept that the gap is not a defect in the game.
The gap is the place where the game continues.

## Generative Questions

1. Can revelation bandwidth be measured in a tradition by comparing doctrinal
   fixity, ritual density, interpretive variance, and institutional coercion?
2. What is the minimum shared attractor required to prevent cultural dissolution
   in a plural society?
3. How much heresy can a tradition metabolize before it fragments?
4. How much law can a tradition accumulate before law stops preserving novelty?
5. Can prophecy be modeled as a perturbation that raises entropy in crystallized
   regimes and lowers entropy in dissolved regimes?
6. What distinguishes a messianic interface from a false-prophet attractor in
   purely game-theoretic terms?
7. Why do durable traditions often combine public ritual with private mystery?
8. Can prayer be formalized as local-policy updating under hypothesized global
   attention?
9. When does apocalypse function as anti-crystallization, and when does it become
   terminal lock-in?
10. What would an institution look like if it were designed to preserve its
    founding revelation while making its own capture difficult?
11. Could AI systems become local novelty harvests, global memory structures, or
    dangerous counterfeit interfaces?
12. Is secular modernity unstable because it lacks metaphysics, or because its
    metaphysics are hidden inside institutions and metrics?
13. What is the optimal mix of text, ritual, law, mysticism, skepticism, and
    marginal dissent for long-run civilizational persistence?
14. If the global and local layers are parts of one system, is "salvation" best
    understood not as escape from the game, but as keeping the game capable of
    future-bearing love?
